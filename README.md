# PX Clothing

**Clothing, Tattoos & Character Appearance for FiveM**

**ESX | QBCore | QBox**

Give players a dedicated place to build their look. PX Clothing brings clothing, hairstyles and accessories into a boutique-style menu, with separate tattoo studios and a hospital appearance clinic. Browse items, preview changes on your character and complete your purchase with cash or bank.

## Features

- **Visual clothing menu** — Browse clothing and accessories by category, search the catalog and select styles and textures with item thumbnails and live character previews.
- **Outfit management** — Save your favorite combinations, load saved outfits and remove the ones you no longer need.
- **Hair & appearance** — Choose hairstyles, hair colors and facial overlays. Face & Body and Face Adjustment are available at the hospital clinic.
- **Dedicated tattoo shops** — Browse tattoos by body area, inspect available artwork previews and try designs on your character before adding them to your purchase.
- **Character camera** — Rotate, move, zoom and focus the preview while your character holds a stationary pose.
- **Shop interactions** — Open the menu through ox_target assistants, with purchase and service feedback delivered through ox_lib notifications.
- **Configurable settings** — Adjust locations, prices, available services and language through config.lua.
- **Five languages included** — English, Serbian, German, Spanish and French.

Includes **14 clothing stores, 6 tattoo studios and 1 hospital appearance clinic**, with locations configurable to suit your server.

## Dependencies

- **ox_lib**
- **ox_target**
- **oxmysql** with a configured MySQL/MariaDB database
- **OneSync** enabled
- **One supported framework setup:**

| Framework | Core | Appearance resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ESX | es_extended | skinchanger + esx_skin |
| QBCore | qb-core | illenium-appearance |
| QBox | qbx_core | illenium-appearance |

Requires an existing freemode character with a saved appearance. Keep your character creation and login appearance system enabled.
